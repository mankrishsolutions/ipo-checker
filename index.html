How it works

Four areas are scored from 0 to 100, then combined into one score.

  • Market demand (35%): GMP as a share of the price band, subscription multiples, institutional bidding.
  • Company financials (30%): revenue and profit growth, profit margin, return on equity, debt.
  • Valuation (20%): P/E, price to book, and P/E against profit growth.
  • News and reviews (15%): tone of recent headlines and how many say subscribe or avoid.
  • 65 and above: Apply. 45 to 64: Maybe. Below 45: Skip. If a source has no data, its area is left out and confidence drops.
