How scoring works

Every score starts at 50 and moves with what the web says.

  • GMP as a percentage of the upper price band: up to +30 or -25.
  • Total subscription multiple: up to +15, or -10 if under-subscribed.
  • Valuation: P/E under 20 and ROE over 20% add points; P/E over 40 or debt/equity over 1 subtracts.
  • Recent news headlines: positive versus cautious tone, up to +/-10.
  • 65 and above: Apply. 45 to 64: Maybe. Below 45: Skip. The green and grey ticks on each card show which sources answered.
